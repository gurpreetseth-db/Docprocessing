How it joins & what it protects

Relationships

fact.client_key = dim_client.client_key
fact.coordinator_key = dim_care_coordinator.coordinator_key
fact.{referral,service_start,review}_date = dim_date.date_key
fact.region = dim_region.region
fact.funder = dim_funder.funder
bridge.plan_key = fact_service_plan.plan_key

🔒 De-identification (client PII)

  • Clients keyed by client_key = sha2(nhi) — no NHI, names, address, phone, email or emergency contacts reach Gold.
  • Date of birth → age + age_band only.
  • Care-coordinator & GP names retained (staff / provider) for workforce and provider analysis.
  • Silver keeps the full record for operational/audit use; Gold is the shared analytics boundary.
